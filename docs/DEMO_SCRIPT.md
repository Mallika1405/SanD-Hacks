# Somewhere: 3-minute demo video script

About 400 spoken words. Record in short clips and edit together; every switch of profile costs time, so use Jordan for most of it.

Before recording: run `npm run seed`, restart the dev server, allow the microphone in Chrome, run one Find care search for your city (so results are instant), and pick Juno's voice on the Voices page.

## 0:00 to 0:22, The problem (stat cards over a dark background, then fade to the app)

**Voiceover**
"One in five US adults has an anxiety disorder in a given year. Worldwide, anxiety is the most common mental disorder, around 359 million people. And in the US, only about half of adults with a mental illness got any treatment last year. Panic doesn't wait for office hours. It hits at 3am, when no one is there, and most apps hand you a form to fill out when you can't think."

**On screen (one card per stat, source in small text)**
- "19.1% of US adults have an anxiety disorder each year." NIMH
- "359 million people worldwide." WHO, 2021
- "Only 50.6% of US adults with a mental illness received treatment." NIMH, 2022

## 0:22 to 0:30, The answer

**Voiceover**
"Meet Somewhere: a voice-first companion for panic, intrusive thoughts and overwhelm, that adapts to each person."

**Screen:** login page, tap Jordan, land on Today (big help card, calm layout).

## 0:30 to 1:25, Panic, in the moment (the core of the demo)

**Screen and voiceover**
1. Tap "I need a hand right now", then "Tell me in my own words". Say aloud: *"My heart is racing and I can't stop shaking."*
   VO: "I just say what's happening. No forms."
2. Juno answers in a calm voice and reflects what you said. Tap **Walk me through breathing**. Let about 15 seconds play, with the "Breathe in / Slowly out" label.
   VO: "A live ElevenLabs voice agent, powered by Gemini, counts every breath with me."
3. Say: *"I'm still panicking."* Juno changes approach instead of repeating itself.
4. Open **More, Sources**.
   VO: "Its guidance comes from a research playbook. Bright Data read NHS and other health pages, we pulled studies from PubMed, Gemini wrote the steps, and Neon stores it all, with every source."
5. Say: *"My chest hurts."* The 911 and 988 screen appears.
   VO: "And safety beats smoothness: chest pain is never treated as anxiety."

## 1:25 to 1:55, A diary that talks back

**Screen:** Diary, tap "Write instead", type one sentence about a rough day, show Juno's gentle reply, then **Read my words** on a saved entry. Cut to Sam's Today with the flagged entry (988, 911, note for the therapist).

**Voiceover**
"The diary is a conversation, by voice or text, and keeps my own words. If something sounds serious, it shows 988, 911, and a note I can send my therapist."

## 1:55 to 2:25, Finding real care

**Screen:** Find care, "A specific therapist", use location, show a therapist card (clinic, distance, phone), then the Clinics tab and Support groups tab.

**Voiceover**
"Find care uses Bright Data and Google Maps to find named therapists near me, the clinic they work for, and support groups, all labeled unverified so I know to check."

## 2:25 to 2:45, It adapts to you

**Screen:** Voices page (play a calming voice, show the cloned "your voice"), then flip to dark mode, then You, "why it's set up this way".

**Voiceover**
"I can pick a calming voice, even my own. The layout, tone and advice change for panic, OCD, worry or burnout, based on research, not guesses."

## 2:45 to 3:00, Close

**Screen:** You, Conversations with Juno, Delete my data.

**Voiceover**
"Private by default. Honest about what it doesn't know. Not a medical service. Somewhere, built with ElevenLabs, Gemini, Neon and Bright Data."

## Sources for the stat cards (check each on the source page before you publish)

- NIMH, Any Anxiety Disorder: 19.1% of US adults in the past year, 31.1% lifetime. https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/statistics/any-anxiety-disorder (note: NIMH says this comes from older survey data, so say "NIMH estimates")
- NIMH, Mental Illness: in 2022, 23.1% of US adults had any mental illness (59.3 million), and 50.6% of them received treatment in the past year. https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/statistics/mental-illness
- WHO, Anxiety disorders fact sheet: 359 million people in 2021. https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/anxiety-disorders (the figure was 301 million in older versions, so confirm which one the page shows on the day you record)
- NIMH, Panic Disorder (optional extra stat): 2.7% of US adults in the past year. https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/statistics/panic-disorder
